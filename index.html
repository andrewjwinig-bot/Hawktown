When to do what

It happens earlier than anyone expects — most leases for next year are signed in September and October.

Late Aug
Lock your group

Have the roommate conversation in the first weeks back. Group size sets everything else, and this is the part that stalls people.

September
Tour 3–4 houses

Walk them back to back on one warm afternoon. Don't wait for October to start looking — the five-bedrooms go first.

Sept – Oct
Sign for next year

Yes, a full year ahead. Most houses are committed by mid-fall. All roommates plus guarantors sign — loop your parents in before you tour, not after.

June – Aug
Move in

Keys in June on most houses — drop your stuff off early and be settled before Musikfest takes over the South Side.

01

Already furnished

Beds, desks, couches, kitchen fully stocked with appliances. Move in with a car load, not a U-Haul — and no hauling anything up the hill.

02

Hundreds less every month

Per-person rent on every listing, plus your split of utilities. No application fee, no broker, no mystery charges in August.

03

Someone actually picks up

Heat out during a February cold snap? You get a real person, not a portal ticket. Rent splits online so nobody's chasing Venmo the week before finals.

This house rental agreement (hereinafter “Lease”) is entered into this {{ f.signDate }} by and between Michael L. Schonberger, Esq., 133 Heather Rd., Suite 132, Bala Cynwyd, PA 19004, hereinafter Landlord, and {{ f.tenantNames }}, Tenants. (Largest number of occupants allowed under this lease is {{ f.occupants }}.)

The Landlord, in consideration of the rents and the following covenants, leases to the Tenants the house at {{ f.house }}, Bethlehem, PA 18015 ({{ f.beds }} bedrooms, {{ f.baths }} baths), subject to the terms and conditions contained in this lease agreement.

1. Rent

Base rent shall be {{ f.rent }} per month × 12 months for a total of {{ f.annual }} ({{ f.perPerson }} per tenant per month if divided equally by all tenants). Rent is due on the 1st day of each month; any rent not received by the 5th is late and a late fee will be assessed. If rent remains unpaid beyond the 15th the late fee shall increase.

2. Term

This lease agreement shall begin {{ f.startDate }} and shall end {{ f.endDate }}.

3. Renewal

If the Tenants do not sign a new lease agreement by {{ f.renewalDate }}, the Landlord will begin to show the house to prospective new tenants. The current Tenants may renew after that date if the house is still available.

4. Utilities

Tenants shall pay {{ f.utilities }} monthly ({{ f.utilPer }} per person) to Landlord for estimated gas/heat, electric, water/sewer usage and trash collection. If total actual usage is less than $4,500.00 during the term, Landlord will add the balance to the security deposit; if in excess, Landlord will deduct it. Tenants pay cable, security monitoring and Wi-Fi directly to providers.

5. Furnishings

Landlord will provide {{ f.beds }} full size beds, dressers, some living room furniture, a 50+ inch smart TV, a kitchen dining set, and all major appliances. Landlord-provided furniture may not be moved to the basement or outside.

6. Smoke Detectors

Smoke and CO detectors are installed as required by law and the City of Bethlehem, and a fire extinguisher is provided in the kitchen. Tenants agree to test detectors regularly, keep fresh batteries in each, and notify Landlord immediately if any is missing or not working.

7. Sale of Property

If a sale takes place, Landlord will give written notice of the new Landlord’s name, address and telephone number, where rent is payable, and confirmation that the security deposit and all obligations under this Lease have been assumed.

8. No Pets

Tenants will not keep any pets on the property without first getting Landlord’s and current tenants’ written permission. If written permission is given there will be a nonrefundable pet fee of {{ f.petFee }}.
9. Tenants’ Promises

Tenants agree to comply with Pennsylvania building and housing codes; keep the property clean and safe; collect and dispose of trash regularly; not deface walls, woodwork, rugs or furnishings; not disturb the neighborhood with trash or excessive noise; keep cushioned furniture off porches; stay off the roofs; and burn no candles or open flames in the house. Tenants will give Landlord access at reasonable hours with reasonable notice, make no alterations without written consent, promptly report needed repairs, and vacate and clean the house by the last day of the term. Landlord is not responsible for property damaged, lost or stolen — renters insurance is advised.

10. Landlord’s Promises

Landlord agrees to maintain the property and common areas to code; maintain the roof, windows, doors, locks, floors, steps, porches, walls, ceilings and structure in good repair; maintain the lawn and clear snow and ice from the front sidewalk; keep all electrical, plumbing, heating and ventilating systems and provided appliances in safe working order; provide drinkable hot water and heat for the entire term; provide new and rekeyed locks; provide exterminating services; and give reasonable notice before entering except in an emergency.

11. Security Deposit

Tenants shall pay at time of lease signing a security deposit of {{ f.deposit }} ({{ f.depPer }} per Tenant). The deposit may not be used as last month’s rent. Landlord will provide a written list of deductions and a detailed accounting of actual utility usage, and refund any balance within 30 days of move-out and receipt of Tenants’ new addresses in writing.

12. Last Month’s Rent

Tenants shall pay last month’s rent and estimated utility usage in advance on or before {{ f.lastRentDate }}.

13. Subleasing and Assignments

The Tenants will not assign this lease or sublet the leased property, or any part of the property.

14. Lease Guarantee

All Tenants shall have one or both parents sign a guarantee to this lease agreement and return it within 30 days of the lease signing. These guarantees become part of the Lease Agreement.

16. Special Clauses

a. All needed cleaning, painting and repairs will be completed during the summer months and complete on or before {{ f.repairDate }}.

b. Tenants will sign the ADDENDUM TO RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENTS as required by Article II, Section E. of the Regulated Rental Unit Occupancy Ordinance of the City of Bethlehem.
